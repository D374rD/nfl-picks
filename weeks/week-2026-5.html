Before you bet

  • You need to be 21 or older and physically in a state where DraftKings Sportsbook is legal.
  • Download the DraftKings Sportsbook app, create an account, verify your identity, and add money under Deposit.
  • New accounts often come with a sign-up offer paid in bonus bets. Read the terms on the offer.

Reading the odds

  • Minus odds, like -115: the favorite side. You bet $11.50 to win $10.
  • Plus odds, like +170: the underdog side. A $10 bet wins $17.
  • If you win, you get your bet back plus the winnings.
  • "Better odds" means you win more for the same bet: a higher number for plus odds (+180 beats +170), or closer to zero for minus odds (-110 beats -120).

The three main bet types

  • Moneyline: pick who wins the game.
  • Spread: a team with + points can lose by less than that number and still win you the bet. A team with - points has to win by more than that number.
  • Total (over/under): bet whether both teams’ points added together go over or under the number.
  • Player props: bet on one player’s stats, like whether a receiver gets over or under 60.5 receiving yards.

Placing a bet

  1. Tap NFL. Each game shows the away team on top, the home team underneath, and three columns: Spread, Total, and Moneyline.
  2. Tap the button for the bet you want. It goes into your bet slip.
  3. Enter your amount as a single (straight) bet. If you add several picks, give each one its own amount. Leave the parlay box empty.
  4. Tap Place Bet. Your bets are listed under My Bets.

Other apps on this page

  • FanDuel works like DraftKings. The steps are almost the same: tap NFL, find the game, tap the price.
  • Kalshi and Polymarket US are prediction markets, not sportsbooks. Instead of betting against the house, you buy a “Yes” share from another user. The price is in cents (a 40¢ share pays $1 if it hits). This page converts those prices to regular odds so you can compare. Polymarket US is app-only, and the Polymarket prices here come from the global Polymarket market, which the US app usually tracks closely, so check the price in the app.
  • Whether each app is available depends on your state, and the rules for prediction markets are still being fought over in court and in state legislatures. Check that an app is legal where you are before signing up.
  • Different apps often pay different amounts for the same bet. Taking the best price is one of the easiest ways to win more over time.

Bonus bets

When you use a bonus bet, you only get the winnings, not the bonus amount back. That makes them worth more on plus-odds picks (like +170) than on minus-odds picks.

Keep it fun

Pick an amount you’re fine losing before the week starts and stick to it. DraftKings has deposit and time limits under Responsible Gaming in your account settings. If betting stops being fun, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
